# Supported grow lights

The PPFD 3D visualizer (`custom:ppfd-3d-card`) covers the **full current Spider Farmer
grow-light lineup** — 22 fixtures, grouped by series in the model picker.

## SF series

SF300 · SF600 · SF1000 · SF1000D · SF2000 · SF2000 Pro · SF4000 · SF7000

## G series

G1000W · G1200W · G1500 · G3000 · G4500 · G5000 · G7000 · G8600

## SE series

SE1500 · SE3000 · SE4500 · SE5000 · SE7000 · SE1000W

---

PPFD-by-height curves are built from each light's published PPFD map. **SF300** and
**SF600** publish only a single PAR-at-height value, so their average/edge spread is
estimated rather than measured.
